# Execution Report

Use this report for both completed and blocked assignments.

```markdown
## Outcome

<Completed or blocked, followed by one-sentence result>

## Changes

- `<path or symbol>`: <behavioral change>

## Acceptance evidence

- <criterion>: <code, test, artifact, or inspection evidence>

## Validation

- `<command or procedure>`: <pass, fail, or not run with reason>

## Coordination notes

- Assumptions: <none or list>
- Design/ticket conflicts: <none or exact conflict and evidence>
- Ownership overlap: <none or affected paths>
- Risks and follow-up: <none or list>

## Remaining work or decision

<None, or the exact blocker and the person/role needed to resolve it>
```

Do not claim completion when required validation failed or was skipped without an accepted exception. Distinguish pre-existing failures from failures introduced by the assignment, and include enough output for the coordinating agent to reproduce the result.
